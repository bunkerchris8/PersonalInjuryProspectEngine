# Source Catalog

Reviewed: 2026-08-16

Source strength describes provenance, not whether a fact is favorable. Every material assertion retains the exact source URL, retrieval date, raw identifier or field name, validation state, and freshness date.

## Integrated sources

| Source | Strength | Access and update pattern | Imported fields | Material limitations |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| U.S. Census Geocoding Services | 5 | Credential-free official single-record or batch API; current MAF/TIGER benchmark | Matched public organizational address, latitude, longitude, TIGER line ID | Coordinates are address-range calculations, not parcel surveys. The API does not provide routed driving distance. Unmatched addresses remain in review. Batch requests are limited to 10,000 records. |
| Census ACS 2024 5-year API | 5 | Official API; API key currently required; annual vintage | Place population, civilian employment, median age, aggregate population age 40+, aggregate employed age 45+, relevant occupation percentages, and all used margins of error | ACS is estimated survey data. The available detailed employment table does not isolate workers age 40-44, so the engine reports workers age 45+ rather than imputing age 40+. Unstable estimates are suppressed from scoring. |
| OSHA ITA Form 300A Summary Data | 5 | Official current CSV and historical ZIP archives; streamed row by row | Establishment identity/address, NAICS, average employment, hours, aggregate recordable/DART cases, and denominator-valid rates | Coverage is partial and employer-submitted; OSHA does not validate all counts. Reporting does not establish fault, negligence, a violation, or benefit eligibility. Case-detail files and narratives are deliberately unsupported. Injury counts add no score points. |
| USAspending federal contract awards | 5 | Official paginated API; three-year configurable lookback | Recipient organization name, UEI, public business address, NAICS, award ID and award dates | A federal award is organizational context, not an endorsement or evidence of legal need. Dollar values and descriptions are not imported. The API can cap broad result sets; use narrower dates or one city per query when warned. Only the newest bounded set of award sources per UEI is retained. |
| Official organization websites | 4 | Manual CSV extraction; review at least every 12 months | Current existence, public business address, role-based channels, leadership role/date, programs, and events | Web pages may be stale or incomplete. Named leaders require a role and observation date. Conflicts are retained for review. |

## Supported manual imports

| Source | Strength | Acquisition | Intended use | Limitations |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| DOL OLMS Online Public Disclosure Room | 5 | Export current filtered/yearly data from `https://olmsapps.dol.gov/olpdr/`, then map it into the organization/contact templates | Union identity, filing number/year, officers, membership or receipts where legitimately reported | Export links are generated by the disclosure application and are not treated as stable URLs. OLMS lacks jurisdiction over unions composed solely of state, county, or municipal employees; absence means not found, not not-a-union. |
| Massachusetts public contracts, licenses, permits, prevailing-wage and labor records | 5 | Official download/API when available; otherwise official document extraction into templates | Contractors, public projects, apprenticeship programs, municipal organizations, current licenses | Programs are spread across state and municipal systems. A project or license is organizational context, not evidence of legal need or misconduct. |
| Official municipal directories and calendars | 4 or 5 | Official page/document import | Departments, public organizational contacts, public meetings and events | Event freshness is 90 days or future-dated. Attendance and material placement still require permission. |
| Chambers, accredited schools, established local news and trade publications | 3 | Manual discovery/corroboration import | Prospect discovery and independent corroboration | Important contact fields require one current Strength 4/5 source or two independent Strength 3 sources. |
| General maps, business directories and event aggregators | 2 | Research queue or discovery import | Location hints and candidate discovery | Not final validation. Strength 2 alone is not used for scoring. |
| Unsourced lists, snippets, scraped lead lists, personal social posts and unclear sources | 1 | Quarantine only | Possible research lead | Never used for automated scoring and never promoted without stronger validation. Data-broker personal information is prohibited. |

## Freshness defaults

| Fact type | Freshness window |
|---|---:|
| Future event or program date | Upcoming until date; recent for 90 days afterward |
| Leadership/contact page | 12 months |
| Union filing | 24 months preferred |
| OSHA summary/inspection context | 36 months; multiple years preferred |
| Federal public contract | 24 months |
| ACS estimate | Latest available five-year vintage; 36-month software window |
| License | 12 months or current official status |
| Permit/public contract | 24 months unless the project status says otherwise |

These defaults flag review needs; they do not silently delete historical assertions.
